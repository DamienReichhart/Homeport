# Homeport Korean (ko) terms

Style: sentences in 합니다/하세요; confirmations as "…하시겠습니까?"; buttons and menus as short nouns
("삭제", "다시 배포", "시작"). No exclamation marks. Korean has no grammatical gender or obligatory plural;
plurals use only `other`.

Particles after an argument: never write 을/를, 이/가, 은/는, 와/과, (으)로 straight after `{name}`;
put a noun after the argument instead ("{name} 컨테이너를", "{host} 호스트에") or use particles that
don't depend on the final sound (에, 에서, 의, 도, 까지, 부터, 에게).
