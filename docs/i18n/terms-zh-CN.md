# zh-CN terms (Simplified Chinese, Mainland)

Conventions:

- Address: no pronoun where possible; 你 when a sentence needs one; 您 avoided.
- Spacing rule: one ASCII space between Chinese characters and Latin words, digits or `{argument}`s (例：`在 {host} 上`、`Docker Engine 未运行`); no space next to full-width punctuation.
- Full-width punctuation in Chinese text (，。：；？（）“”); ASCII inside code, commands, paths and arguments. Keep `…` (single U+2026) where English has it; keep `·` separators.
- Plurals: only `other` (`{count, plural, other {# 个镜像}}`); `=0` kept when English has it.
- Chinese has no gender/plural inflection; measure words: 个 (most), 台 (machines, not used for Hosts), 条 (events, log lines, rules).
