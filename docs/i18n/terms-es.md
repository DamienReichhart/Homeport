# Homeport — términos en español (`es`)

Neutral international Spanish. Address: **tú** (informal), as current Windows and macOS do in Spanish
("¿Quieres eliminar…?", "Inicia el Engine"). Never _usted_, _vos_ or _vosotros_; when English addresses
several people, rephrase impersonally. Buttons and menu items: infinitive ("Eliminar", "Volver a
desplegar"). Titles of confirm dialogs: question with ¿…? ("¿Eliminar {name}?"). No exclamation marks.

Capitalisation: English loanwords that are product terms keep English form (Engine, Swarm, Kubernetes
objects such as Pod, Node, Service, Secret, Namespace); other loanwords are lowercase Spanish nouns
(host, stack, release, chart). Spanish words follow Spanish capitalisation (lowercase in a sentence).
