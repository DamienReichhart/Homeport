# Trademark policy

Homeport's code is free software under the [GNU AGPL, version 3](LICENSE). Its **name** is not: "Homeport", and the logos in [`assets/brand/`](assets/brand), belong to Damien Reichhart and identify the official project. The licence lets you copy, change and share the code; this policy says how you may use the name and logo, so that people always know which Homeport they're running.

## What you may do without asking

- **Redistribute unmodified builds** of Homeport, under its name and logo, from the official releases or built from an unmodified tag.
- **Package Homeport for a distribution** (a Linux distribution, Homebrew, winget, Scoop, the AUR, Flathub, Snap…) under its name, with the minimal patches packaging needs: build flags, paths, dependency versions, disabling the in-app updater. Say in the package that it's a community package, if it is.
- **Say that something works with Homeport**, like "a Homeport plugin" or "tested with Homeport", as long as it doesn't suggest the project made or endorses it.
- **Write about Homeport**: articles, tutorials, videos, talks, reviews, comparisons, using its name and logo to refer to it.

## What needs written permission

- **Naming a fork or another product "Homeport"**, or a name likely to be confused with it ("Homeport Pro", "HomePort Desktop"…).
- **Using the logo** for a fork, a modified build, or another product.
- **Implying endorsement** by the project or its maintainer, or that a modified build is the official one.
- **Selling** anything under the Homeport name, including builds of it.

## Forks

Forks are welcome; the licence allows them. A fork that changes more than packaging needs must be renamed and must replace the logos and icons (`assets/brand/`, `src-tauri/icons/`, `website/public/`) with its own. It may say it is "based on Homeport" or "a fork of Homeport".

## Other names

Docker, Podman, Kubernetes, Helm, Nomad, containerd, Incus, Colima, OrbStack and Rancher Desktop are trademarks of their respective owners. Homeport is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them. Homeport names them only to say what it works with.

## Questions

Ask in an [issue](https://github.com/DamienReichhart/Homeport/issues) before you use the name in a way this page doesn't cover.
